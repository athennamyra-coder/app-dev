## My Favorite Series 🌅

**Title:** *The Summer I Turned Pretty*  
**Genre:** Romance, Coming-of-Age, Drama  
**Created by:** Jenny Han  

### 🌻 Why I Love It
I love *The Summer I Turned Pretty* because it beautifully captures the emotions of growing up, first love, and friendship. The characters feel real, and the beach setting gives it such a warm and nostalgic vibe.  

> “Sometimes you really don’t see something until it’s gone.”  

### 💫 Favorite Characters
- **Belly Conklin** – sweet and relatable  
- **Conrad Fisher** – mysterious and deep  
- **Jeremiah Fisher** – kind and charming  

### 💕 My Thoughts
This series reminds me that love can be both exciting and complicated, and that summer memories last forever.
